# DBLABS — Brand Voice & Personality

> AI that makes you laugh, not cry.
> Vibe Coded Entertainment.

DBLABS is a studio that builds consumer apps using AI to create **joy, rivalry, discovery, and human connection** — not more productivity guilt. Think Channel 4 meets a Y Combinator startup. The products include:

- **Villain** — competitive accountability. AI pairs you against a rival with the same goals.
- **Scout** — curiosity-driven running and walking. Narrates your city like a documentary.
- **Debt** — gamified promise-keeper with real stakes.
- **Arena** — AI-powered real-life challenge generator inspired by Taskmaster.

## Audience
Curious, ambitious, social **22–35** year-olds in the **UK and US**. Self-aware enough to find productivity culture funny. They want to grow but also want to have a good time doing it.

## Personality dial
- **Bold** — confident, declarative, never hedged.
- **Playful** — finds the absurd in the everyday and points at it.
- **Slightly irreverent** — pokes the productivity industry in the ribs.
- **Smart, never corporate.** Fun, never childish. Confident without taking itself too seriously.

## Voice — the do/don't table

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| "Go do something." | "Discover personalised recommendations." |
| "Your rival just lapped you." | "You've fallen behind your goal." |
| "Pay up. Or do the thing." | "Don't forget to complete your task!" |
| Short sentences. Verbs first. | Marketing throat-clearing. |
| Specific, sensory, slightly weird. | Vague platitudes. |
| Talk like one human to another. | Talk like a brand. |

## Anti-patterns (the AI-startup tropes to avoid)
- Purples and electric-blue gradients
- "Sparkle" / wand iconography
- "AI-powered" anything in copy
- Soft pastels and rounded everything
- Empty-promise verbs: "unlock", "empower", "transform"
- Stock 3D blobs, isometric illustrations
- Long onboarding paragraphs
- Em-dashes used like nervous tics
- "Hey there, friend!" energy

## Sample lines (paste-able, on-brand)

- **Headline (hero):** Go do something.
- **Subhead:** Real-world quests, near you. Swipe, accept, do, brag.
- **Empty state:** Nothing here yet. That's a you problem.
- **Push:** Your rival just hit the gym. Cute.
- **Error:** That didn't work. Try the other thing.
- **Onboarding nudge:** Pick one. You can change it later. Probably won't.
- **Success:** Receipts uploaded. Bragging unlocked.

## Visual language (top-level)
- **Cream backgrounds**, deep ink, **loud lime** as the studio accent.
- **2.5px ink borders** on everything that matters, plus a **hard offset shadow** (5px 5px 0) for tappable surfaces.
- Display type in **Space Grotesk** (or close grotesque), body in **Inter**, all-caps tickers in **JetBrains Mono**.
- Heavy use of pill shapes, ticker rows, hand-printed numerals, sticker badges.
- Imagery is **photographic and a little chaotic** — phones in real hands, runners in real streets, receipts on real tables. Not 3D blobs, not stock startup hero illustrations.

## House of brands
Each app gets its own primary color and headline-vibe; everything else (type, layout, borders, shadows, pill chrome, ticker rows) stays consistent so the family resemblance reads instantly.
